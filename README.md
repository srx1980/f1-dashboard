# F1 Dashboard

A clean, minimal Formula 1 dashboard covering the full 2026 season on a single page. Follow the championship as it unfolds — standings, race results, the full calendar, and post-race summaries, all in one place. No frameworks, no backend, pure static HTML/CSS/JS.

## What's inside

- **Upcoming Race** — circuit details, live countdown to lights out, and the weekend schedule
- **Season Standings** — driver and constructor championship tables side by side
- **Latest Race Results** — full classification with winner highlight and DNF tracking
- **Season Calendar** — every Grand Prix of 2026 with winners and race status
- **Race Summary** — a post-race recap of the latest Grand Prix

## Explore more

Beyond the main dashboard, the site also includes dedicated pages for every driver and team on the grid, plus an overview of the 2026 technical regulations — the biggest rule change in a generation.
